Privacy

How we handle your details

Morgan Richards Garage Ltd (company no. 07146809, Unit 12 Anchorage Point, Anchor & Hope Lane, Charlton, London SE7 7SQ) is the data controller for information you send through this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, phone number, and — if you choose to give them — your email address, vehicle registration, preferred day, and the message describing what you need.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to arrange the work. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

How it reaches us

The form is delivered to our email inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service acting on our behalf. Your submission passes through their systems in transit.

Other services on our site

Our homepage embeds a Google Map so you can find us. Google may set cookies and receive your IP address when that map loads. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call 020 8854 2550 or email morganrichardsgarage@yahoo.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
